# User Manual

Audience: CSPC students and boarding house owners/landlords.

---

## Part 1 — For Students

### 1.1 Getting Started
- Visit the system's public landing page — no account needed to browse accredited listings, view photos, prices, amenities, ratings, and the map of all listings.
- Booking, reservations, and payments require logging in with your **CSPC institutional Google account** (`@my.cspc.edu.ph`) via "Continue with Google."
- On first login, complete your profile (student ID, course, year/section, sex, emergency contact, guardian name) — this is required before you can book, and pre-fills into every future reservation.

### 1.2 Finding a Boarding House
- Use the **List View / Map View** toggle to browse listings as cards, or see every accredited property pinned on one map.
- On the Student Dashboard, filter by price range, star rating, and search by name/location/owner.
- Open a listing to see full details, photo gallery, tenant reviews, and its exact location relative to campus (walking directions, from either your current GPS location or from the CSPC gate).

### 1.3 Making a Reservation
1. From a listing, select a room (only rooms matching your registered sex, or unisex/mixed rooms, are selectable).
2. Choose your check-in date and number of months.
3. Choose a payment method: **Cash** (pay the owner directly at move-in) or **Online (GCash/Card)** via PayMongo.
4. Submit. Your request is now **Pending Review** — the owner must approve it.

**One reservation at a time:** you cannot submit a new booking while you have an active occupancy or a confirmed booking elsewhere. Cancel or complete your current stay first.

### 1.4 While Waiting for Approval
Your **My Bookings** page shows the request's status and your requested check-in date. You may **Cancel Reservation** at any time while it's pending.

### 1.5 After Approval
- **Cash bookings** are immediately confirmed and you're marked as an active resident.
- **Online (PayMongo) bookings** require you to complete payment after approval — a **Pay Move-In Cost** button appears. Until payment clears, the booking shows **"Reserved — Awaiting Payment"** and does **not** count as an active occupancy yet. You may still **cancel** at this stage if you change your mind, since nothing has been finalized.
- Once payment settles, you can **Print Receipt** for your records.

### 1.6 During Your Stay
- **Request Extension** from My Bookings to ask for additional months — the owner must approve it.
- Leave a **rating and review** for your boarding house from its listing page.
- Update your profile (contact number, emergency contact, photo) any time from **Profile**.

### 1.7 Notifications
You'll receive in-app notifications for: booking submission confirmation, approval/rejection, payment settlement, and extension decisions.

### 1.8 Installing as an App
On both desktop and mobile browsers, you can install this system to your home screen/desktop like a native app ("Add to Home Screen" / the browser's install prompt) — it will continue to show previously-loaded listings even with a poor or no internet connection.

## Part 2 — For Boarding House Owners

### 2.1 Registering
1. Register an owner account (email/password, or Google sign-in with any email).
2. Complete your onboarding profile with contact details.
3. Submit your property's accreditation application: name, description, address, GPS pin (required — drag the map pin, use "Use GPS," or search an address and select a suggestion; typing an address without selecting a suggestion will **not** move the pin and is blocked at submission), photos, price, amenities, and **all 8 required legal documents**:
   - Business Permit Application Form
   - Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
   - DTI Registration Certificate
   - Barangay Business Clearance
   - Certificate of Building Inspection
   - Zoning Certificate / Locational Clearance
   - Sanitary Permit / Health Clearance
   - Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)
4. Submit for SASO admin review. You'll be notified once approved or rejected.

### 2.2 Managing Your Listing
Once accredited, from **Bookings** / **Rooms** / your dashboard you can:
- Add, edit, or remove rooms — set capacity, price, and gender designation per room.
- Update your listing's photos, description, amenities, and pricing at any time.
- Set your move-in cost policy (advance rent months, deposit months, refundable vs. consumable deposit).

### 2.3 Handling Reservation Requests
On **Bookings**, every pending request from a student appears with their details and chosen payment method.
- **Approve** — for a cash booking, the student is immediately seated as an active resident. For an online (PayMongo) booking, approval only reserves the slot; the student still needs to complete payment before they count as an occupant. You'll see this clearly marked as **"Reserved — Awaiting Payment"**, distinct from a fully settled booking.
- **Reject** — the student is notified.
- **Mark Paid** — for cash bookings, once you've physically received payment, mark it here for your own records (this does not affect occupancy, which was already granted on approval).

### 2.4 Room Details Page
Open any room to see every current boarder's full details: reference number, name, age, check-in date, address, contact, emergency contact, guardian name, duration, rent, move-in cost breakdown, payment status, **and how they paid** (Cash / Online) — plus a **Checkout** action to end a resident's stay when they move out, freeing the room.

### 2.5 Document Renewals
When a permit is nearing expiry, you'll receive a warning notification. Go to **Documents** to upload a renewed file before it expires:
- Letting one of the **4 critical permits** (Business Permit, Barangay Clearance, Sanitary Permit, Fire Safety) lapse will **automatically archive your listing**, removing it from public view until resolved.
- The other **4 supplementary permits** (Cedula, DTI, Building Inspection, Zoning) only trigger a warning notification — they do not auto-archive your listing.

### 2.6 Stay Extensions
When a student requests an extension, you'll see it flagged on that booking in **Bookings** — approve or reject it directly there.

### 2.7 Notifications
You'll be notified of: new reservation requests, document expiry warnings, and accreditation status changes.
